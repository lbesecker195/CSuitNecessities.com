Curv isn’t marketing

Curv is a self-reinforced polypropylene composite — it takes a hit, deforms, and recovers, where polycarbonate can crack and ABS can stay dented. For checked use especially, that resilience shows over years. Frequent travelers buy the Cosmolite on Amazon.

Handles, wheels, interior

The telescoping handle is rigid with minimal play; wheels are smooth. Interior is a straightforward clamshell with a divider. Compare the carry-on and checked sizes on the Amazon listing.

Before you buy — quick answers

How light is it, exactly?
The spinner carry-on is roughly 4.4 lb and the medium checked around 6 lb — among the lightest hard shells available. Current weights per size are on the Amazon page.
Does Curv really resist cracking?
Yes — it’s designed to flex and recover. It’ll scuff, but structural cracks are far less common than with polycarbonate.
