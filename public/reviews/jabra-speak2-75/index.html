Full-duplex is the real upgrade

Cheap speakerphones are half-duplex — when the far side talks, your mic ducks, so interruptions and quick agreement get lost. This one is full-duplex, so the conversation flows naturally. It also normalizes levels so the person leaning back isn’t inaudible. Road warriors buy one on Amazon and leave it in the bag permanently.

Connectivity and battery

USB-C for a guaranteed-clean laptop connection, Bluetooth for a phone, ~32 hours of talk time, and a hard-shell pouch. Check the current bundle and any dongle inclusion on the Amazon listing.
