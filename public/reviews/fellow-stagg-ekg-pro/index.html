Temperature is a real variable

Green tea wants ~175°F, a light-roast pour-over ~205°F, black tea a full boil. A one-button kettle can’t do that. The Stagg EKG Pro lets you set exactly what the leaf or bean wants and holds it while you’re on a call. Tea and coffee people check the Amazon price.

The pour is controllable

The gooseneck is restricted so you get a thin, steady, aimable stream — which is the whole game in pour-over and matters for blooming tea too. The counterweighted handle keeps it balanced as it empties. Ritual-minded drinkers buy it on Amazon.

Pro vs standard EKG

The Pro adds altitude adjustment (so ‘boiling’ is actually right where you are), a sharper display and a few presets. See which model is listed and the current price on the Amazon listing.
