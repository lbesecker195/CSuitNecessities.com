Where each one wins

Tumi Alpha 3 Continental Dual Access Carry-On

The most travel-ready: FXT ballistic nylon that flexes and survives, dual top/front access, suiter brackets, and the Tracer recovery program. Heavy for a soft-side, and on the larger end of carry-on legal. Check the current Amazon price.

Rimowa Original Cabin (Aluminum)

The status object. Anodized aluminum that looks superb and then collects dents as a feature. Heavier and pricier; 5-year warranty and a global service network. Check the current Amazon price.

Briggs & Riley Baseline Essential Carry-On

The pragmatist’s flex: an unconditional lifetime warranty that covers airline damage, plus the Outsider handle that frees all interior space and CX compression for overpackers. Check the current Amazon price.

The call

For pure function, the Tumi Alpha 3 wins — toughest shell, best organization, most travel-ready design. Buy the Briggs & Riley if warranty peace of mind matters more to you than anything else; its lifetime coverage is the real deal. The Rimowa is a status purchase — beautiful, heavier, and it will dent.

Before you buy — quick answers

Which survives checked-bag abuse best?
The Tumi’s ballistic nylon flexes and recovers; the Rimowa dents; the Briggs & Riley is tough and, if something breaks, free to fix forever.
Lightest of the three?
The Briggs & Riley Baseline is typically the lightest to lift, thanks to the external handle design.

The most travel-ready choice is the Tumi Alpha 3.
